    Studio

    I am Jonathan Coles, a senior designer in Bristol working across UI/UX, digital and 3D visualisation. Twenty-five years of that has been in-house rather than at an agency, which means owning the whole chain: the 3D model, the printed prototype, the dieline, the press-ready file, the storefront it sells on, and the film that launches it.

    Eighteen of those years were with The Somerset Toiletry Co. I led the redesign, build and ongoing maintenance of the e-commerce platform on WordPress and WooCommerce, and took product ranges from a model in Modo to a MakerBot prototype to manufacture. The storefront finished at 100 on desktop and 99 on mobile against Google’s Core Web Vitals, and traffic and revenue rose with it.

    Before that, five years at Codemasters as a 3D artist on Dragon Empires and Maelstrom, building the front-end menu systems for Toca Race Driver 3 and the key art that took thirteen magazine covers in twelve months. I led and mentored a five-person art team. I started at Matinee Sound & Vision, animating the 2001 Williams F1 car launch film.

    I am open to senior design roles and to studio and freelance projects, remote or on site.
